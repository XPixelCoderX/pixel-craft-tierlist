About PCN Tierlist

PCN Tierlist is the competitive ranking hub of Pixel Craft Network (PCSMP). Players are tested and tiered from HT1 to LT5 across 38 Minecraft PvP game modes including Sword, Crystal, Pot, UHC, Mace, Axe and SMP. Rankings update live and can be filtered by region: North America, Europe, South America, Asia, Oceania and Africa. Join the community on Discord and play on play.pcsmp.net.
